C# projects.

Windows forms latency analizer.

I developed a simple quick and small application for windows who probes (ping) a given website each few miliseconds to check the response time, then this information saved and processed to make guesses about if the quality of the network is suitable for things who require reliability, like online gaming or video calls, it is useful for checking if other users of a shared network are using it on a way that will bother you, without invading their privacy.

Windows forms + selenium webscrapper for in real-time wind energy generation

I realized that people worry way too much about electric prices in a given timeframe and have no long-term strategy to tackle both electricity prices and carbon emissions, so I developed a webscrapper to fetch real time information about how much wind energy is part of the energy mix of Ireland's electric grid, the plan was to use this information to automatically run home devices tampering them with ESP32 or Arduino devices, but found no way of comercialize this or find people interested on it, so it ended up as a personal utility who let me know when to run a washing machine or a kettle, I still want to finish it though.
